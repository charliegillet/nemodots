## Flexible deployment options

### Serverless Inference

Get started fast with usage-based consumption of top open-source models via a fully managed inference API in Crusoe Intelligence Foundry.

Best for

• Early-stage workloads

• Low volume traffic

• Rapid experimentation

[Get started](https://console.crusoecloud.com/request-foundry) [Read docs](https://docs.crusoecloud.com/serverless-inference/overview)

### Self-Serve Deployments

Run top models in Crusoe Intelligence Foundry with inference optimized for throughput or responsiveness. Deploy models customized with [Serverless Fine-Tuning](https://www.crusoe.ai/cloud/serverless-fine-tuning) in one click.

Best for

• Production-scale workloads

• Low-latency applications

• Predictable costs

[Get started](https://console.crusoecloud.com/request-foundry) [Read docs](https://docs.crusoecloud.com/self-serve-deployments/overview)

### Tailored Deployments

Work with our team for the highest level of optimization and a dedicated, benchmarked endpoint. You bring the model and define the requirements, we handle the rest.

Best for

• Proprietary models

• Bespoke inference optimization

• SLA-backed performance

[Request benchmarking](https://www.crusoe.ai/managed-inference/tailored-deployment/request)

## Unmatched performance, flexibility, and scalability

1

### Breakthrough speed

Powered by MemoryAlloy technology with a cluster-wide KV cache, Crusoe's inference engine delivers up to 9.9x faster time-to-first-token\* and 5x higher throughput using speculative decoding and dynamic batching.

2

### Ultimate flexibility

Choose the right inference deployment option for your workload. Pay as you go with Serverless Inference APIs, use Self-Serve Deployments to further optimize for throughput or responsiveness, or work with us via Tailored Deployments to customize for your unique needs.

3

### Seamless scaling

Go from experiment to production without the operational burden. We handle the infrastructure to deliver reliable performance even under peak load. Meet changing user demands with elastic scaling that flexes from single-user testing to sustained, high-volume production deployments.

## Frequently  asked questions

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6855c1aa175582ee23e0aa19/6916ec4aed9e2f064d1c3ca6_faq-image.svg)

Can I run inference on my own model?

Yes. Select the [Tailored Deployments](https://www.crusoe.ai/managed-inference/tailored-deployment/request) option to connect with us about using your own model.

You can also now fine-tune open-source models in Crusoe Intelligence Foundry using [Serverless Fine-Tuning](https://www.crusoe.ai/cloud/serverless-fine-tuning) and go live in one click with Self-Serve Deployments.

What if I want a dedicated instance?

Please select the [Tailored Deployments](https://www.crusoe.ai/managed-inference/tailored-deployment/request) option to connect with us about exploring a dedicated instance.

What optimization profiles are available, and how do I choose?

Self-Serve Deployments offers two profiles: throughput, optimized for cost and resource efficiency, and responsiveness, optimized for low latency.

What models are available for Managed Inference?

View all currently supported models in [Crusoe Intelligence Foundry](https://console.crusoecloud.com/request). If the model you need isn't available yet, contact us about [Tailored Deployments](https://www.crusoe.ai/managed-inference/tailored-deployment/request).
