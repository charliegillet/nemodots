# The Recursive Agents Hackathon

San Francisco, CASat, Oct 17 at 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM(PDT)

**Build a team of agents you can walk away from.**

During the PyTorch **Conference in San Francisco**, build agents that can **take a goal and run with it**. Let them make the plan, spin up sub-agents, delegate work, communicate with each other, check their own progress, recover when things break, and keep going.

**Get a head start.** Crusoe is hosting Field Day the day before the hackathon. You'll join a random team and race to take a model from raw data to production. Friday, Oct 16, 1-5pm. Spots are limited, [register here](https://luma.com/pcfayy36)!

Push beyond the one-shot agent into persistent loops that rarely need you involved: **agents building teams and handling delegation, memory, and long-running inference** that can keep operating after you walk away until the work is complete

Build on **Crusoe Managed Inference** and put leading open models, including NVIDIA’s **Nemotron** family, into production on high-performance NVIDIA infrastructure.

Compete to win **$25,000 in cash prizes** and an **NVIDIA RTX 5090.**

**Note:** This event is fully in-person in San Francisco. Teams may have up to four members. All attendees must apply and be approved to attend.
